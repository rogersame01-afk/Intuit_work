Executive Summary

We are seeing some unit growth in the last 16 days for Prospects (index 108) and Winbacks (index 104), but all other segments are down. Starts are down overall (index 95), driven by Returning (index 81) and New Account (index 89) declines, though Prospect and Winback starts grew. S2C is down across all segments and SKUs.

Key Takeaways

  1. Completes index at 88 — down 6,427 units in the prior 16-day window. Veteran Returning accounts for 62% of the total gap.
  2. Credit Karma is offsetting underlying softness: total starts index is 95 with CK, but only 85 excluding it. CK grew from 2,969 to 9,218 starts (index 311).
  3. Post-income funnel is the primary pressure point in the prior 16-day window: I2C declined meaningfully (Veteran Returning index 57, First Year Returning index 64). Full-season YTD I2C is much healthier (Veteran Returning index 90, First Year Returning index 88) — the steep decline is concentrated in the late-season cohort.
  4. Paid Search declined significantly: Generic index 32, Brand index 65. This is the largest source of start decline outside of Non-Campaign (index 74).
  5. Clear SKU migration: standalone Deluxe (index 49) and Premium (index 44) declined in Returning; TTL Deluxe (index 182), TTL Premium (index 144), and Solopreneur (index 652) absorbed the volume.
  6. Prior 16-day window S2C is softer than full-season: window cohort total S2C index 68, but full-season YTD S2C index 96. Late-season starters convert at structurally lower rates, and TY25 is below TY23 at every segment.
