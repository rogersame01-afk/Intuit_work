Methodology

Adapted from the TY23 forecast in awatson4/nlac. Customers with a federal e-file reject are counted as qualifying for a letter if they remain unresolved (no accepted e-file or print-to-mail) 6 days after rejection.

TY24 daily qualifying counts from dlprd.tax_rpt.product_analytics_master were aggregated to months, then scaled by the Feb-Apr TY25/TY24 ratio of 1.0723x to project May through November. The Est Refund / Bal Due split uses the observed TY25 actuals mix (77.0% / 23.0%).
