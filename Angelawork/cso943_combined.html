Methodology

Data Sources

  • Population: cgan_ustax_published.aw_cso_943 — one row per auth_id, 3 cells × campaign/holdout
  • Contacts: ent_care_7216_dwh.rpt_cct_interactions — tax_year=2025, bu='cg', handled legs (customer_handle_flg=1)
  • Refiles: dlprd.tax_rpt.product_analytics_master (pam), tax_year=2025 — first_fed_efile_rejected_date, first_fed_efile_accepted_date, first_print_to_mail_date_adj
  • Send date: Braze canvas 943_CSO_TY25-E-file-Reject-IRS_5k_259k_medium_EM delivered 2026-05-19 (PT), confirmed from dlprd.tax_src.src_braze_turbotax_email_send; only contacts & refiles on/after this date are counted
  • Join key: auth_id (pam joined at tax_year=2025 — one row per auth)

Metric Definitions

  • Contact Rate = distinct auth_ids with ≥1 handled contact ÷ cell population
  • AHT = mean handle_seconds_dur across handled contact legs
  • Refile Success Rate = distinct auth_ids who resolved (fed e-file accepted or printed to mail on/after 2026-05-19) ÷ distinct auth_ids with a federal e-file rejection. All customers in the population had a rejection, so the denominator equals the cell population.

Statistical Tests

  • Contact Rate: two-proportion Z-test (campaign vs. holdout per cell)
  • AHT: Welch's t-test (unequal variance, large-sample normal approximation)
  • Refile Success Rate: two-proportion Z-test (campaign vs. holdout per cell, and pooled across cells)
  • Significance threshold: α = 0.05 (two-tailed)
