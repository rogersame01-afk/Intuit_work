VoC Context — customer pain in this area

During First Peak (Jan 24 – Feb 15, 2026), First Year Returning customers across all five SKUs generated contact volume concentrated in top-of-funnel friction. The highest-pain theme by volume × negativity is E-file Reject / Return Rejected (121 contacts, 66.9% negative), where customers who successfully complete filing encounter a confusing rejection loop with no clear recovery path. Document Upload Issues (W-2 / Tax Docs) is the highest-volume theme (175 contacts, 28.6% negative). Tax Return Amendment (153 contacts, 32.0% negative) spikes in First Peak, with many contacts occurring same-day post-filing.
